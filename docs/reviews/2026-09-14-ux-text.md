# UX and text review, round two (2026-09-14)

Reviewed as a player would use it, on the live site
(https://deradon.github.io/wow-forever-talent-calc/, `last-modified`
2026-09-13 21:19, which includes the spells pages), driven with Playwright at
1600x1000 desktop. Mobile was not looked at, per the owner's decision.

Scope: the round-one fixes listed in `docs/reviews/2026-09-13-consolidated.md`,
the surfaces added by the eight handovers since, and a text sweep of every
player-facing route. 25 findings, worst first. A second opinion on the two
visual calls (the `#/changes` diff, the print view) was taken from codex and
agreed with both independently.

## Verified fixed from round one

Each was exercised on the live site, not read from the code.

| Round-one finding | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Keyboard refund broken | Focus a cell, `Enter` -> rank 2, `Backspace` -> rank 1. `ArrowRight` moves focus within the tree. |
| No feedback for blocked actions | Clicking a locked cell raises a red bar: *"Infusion of Light needs 15 points in Holy."* |
| No "no points left" state | At 51/51 the header reads `Points left: 0 / 51 all spent`; the available ring drops from `rgb(30,255,0)` to `rgb(79,125,70)`; tooltips add *"No talent points left (51 of 51 spent)."* |
| No search, no visible names | `/` focuses the box; "rend" gives `2 matches` plus jump chips (`Improved Rend  Arms, row 1`), non-matching cells dim. |
| Tooltip meta longer than the description | Visible meta is now at most one change line plus one trust line. Everything else is behind the nested terms. Checked across all 52 mage cards. |
| Landing page bloated | Four lines and nine class cards, nothing else. |
| No badge legend | Present in the control row: *"? = uncertain reading, check it. The corner number is rank / max rank."* (incomplete - finding 10). |
| Locked border / small text contrast | Locked cell text `rgb(240,230,210)`; requirement line ~5.5:1; help line ~9:1. All pass. Size, not contrast, is the remaining problem (finding 19). |

Also working and worth recording: undo/redo by button and by `Ctrl+Z` /
`Ctrl+Shift+Z`, the class switcher with Back restoring the build
(`#/mage?v=1&t=001`), the shortcuts overlay, embed mode, the `Copy as text`
export, and the nested tooltip chain itself.

## Findings

### 1. A hand-edited `t=` dumps three developer notice bars on the player — severity: high

`#/warrior?v=1&t=5553253055532530555325305553` renders three stacked amber/red
bars above the trees:

> Ignored points for unknown talents (arms #18, arms #19, arms #20, arms #21,
> arms #22, arms #23, arms #25, arms #26, arms #27, arms #28).

> Clamped ranks above the maximum (Improved Heroic Strike 5 -> 3, Improved Rend
> 5 -> 3, Improved Charge 3 -> 2, ...).

> **Build adjusted.** Build adjusted: 1 point dropped because the link did not
> satisfy the talent rules.
> - arms/anger-management: prereq (needs improved-tactical-mastery 5)

Three separate defects in one place: internal encoding positions (`arms #18`),
internal talent slugs and the abbreviation `prereq`, the engineering word
*Clamped*, and a title that repeats itself verbatim in its own body. Round-one
A1.2 said requirement lines must never leak internal ids; this is the same leak
one layer up. Every truncated or mistyped shared link lands here.

**Suggestion.** One bar, one sentence, no ids: *"This link did not fit the
current trees - 11 points were dropped and 12 talents capped at their Forever
rank. The build below is what fits."* Keep the detail for `#/review/<class>`.

### 2. The print view is not print-ready — severity: high

`print.css` under print emulation (`shots/print.png`): the three tree panels
keep a near-black background on a white page, so a printed build is three large
black rectangles. `Points left: 35 / 51`, `Required level: 25`, `16 of 51
spent, 35 left`, the build URL and the whole rank column (`3/3`, `5/5`) print
in pale gold or light grey on white and are barely legible. The summary list
renders as one narrow column with two thirds of the page blank, not the three
columns the round-2 handover describes. The blue "new" stars survive at full
saturation and are the loudest thing on the sheet. Codex, shown the same image
cold, reached the same conclusion.

**Suggestion.** In `@media print`: force `.tree-grid`/`.tree-panel`
backgrounds to white, promote every gold/grey token to near-black, hide the
change stars, print the build URL at body size, and give the summary the
three-column rule it was meant to have.

### 3. Wowhead import loses most of the build and explains it badly — severity: high

A real Wowhead Classic protection link
(`/classic/talent-calc/embed/warrior/0503--550340510553151`) reports:

> 21 of 51 points placed. Dropped: Improved Charge (fewer ranks in Forever),
> Improved Shield Block (not in Forever), Improved Sunder Armor (fewer ranks in
> Forever), Improved Disarm (fewer ranks in Forever), Improved Taunt (not in
> Forever), Concussion Blow (fewer ranks in Forever), and 6 more. 30 points
> left to spend.

Three problems. *"(fewer ranks in Forever)"* reads as "this talent is gone",
when the talent exists and only the surplus points were dropped. *"and 6
more"* is followed immediately by the full list anyway, so the truncation buys
nothing. And that list is **per point, not per talent** - on the 46-point Arms
link, `Improved Charge`, `Deep Wounds`, `Impale`, `Piercing Howl`, `Improved
Slam` and `Improved Execute` each appear twice with different numbers beside
them.

**Suggestion.** One line per talent with the real reason and the real number:
*"Improved Charge - 2 of 3 points kept (2 ranks in Forever)"*, *"Tactical
Mastery - 3 points lost (not in Forever)"*. Drop the "and N more" truncation
since the list follows.

### 4. The import panel is crammed into the 320 px summary column — severity: high

`Import` opens inline at the bottom of the summary column, so the textarea is
~280 px wide (a pasted Wowhead URL wraps across three lines) and the dropped
list stacks 17 rows into a strip, pushing the page far below the fold. The one
screen in the app that is pure prose is given the narrowest column on the page.

**Suggestion.** Make Import a centred modal, roughly 640 px, reusing the
shortcuts overlay's dialog shell (focus trap and Escape already exist there).

### 5. "Checked by a reviewer." contradicts every global caveat — severity: high

Mage `presence-of-mind`, `incineration` and `pyroblast` end their derivation
with *"Checked by a reviewer."* - correctly, 57 records now carry
`source.reviewed: true`. But the site header still says *"unofficial and
unreviewed"*, the landing page says *"read ... by a local vision model and
**unreviewed**"*, the footer says *"unreviewed"*, and `#/races` and `#/spells`
say *"nothing here is reviewed"*. A player who opens a checked talent is being
told two opposite things on one screen.

**Suggestion.** Replace the blanket word with a number that the build can
compute: *"57 of 470 talents have been checked by hand; the rest are as read."*
One sentence, one place.

### 6. Pipeline vocabulary still reaches player-facing pages — severity: high

Swept every route for the banned words. Survivors:

| Where | Text |
|---|---|
| Landing | "Read from BlizzCon 2026 demo footage by a local **vision model**" |
| Every class page | "**Extracted** from BlizzCon 2026 stream footage (**rank-0** tooltips only); ranks 2+ are **anticipated**" |
| Every footer | "icons and **frame crops** are property of Blizzard Entertainment" |
| Tooltip, `?` card | "The **reading** in use", "**70%**", "Only one **reading** was recorded." |
| Tooltip, trust card | "Read from the stream at 5:59:37, **70% confidence**." |
| `#/spells/<class>` | "Full text (2 **readings**)", "Check this **reading**." |

`rank-0` is the worst of these: players count ranks from 1, so the one sentence
meant to set expectations uses an index they have never seen. The stream
timestamp is acceptable inside the derivation card per the owner's rule; the
`70% confidence` beside it is not.

**Suggestion.** "vision model" -> "read automatically from the video";
"Extracted ... rank-0 tooltips only" -> "Only the first rank of each talent was
on screen; ranks above that are estimated from Classic Era."; "frame crops" ->
"screenshots"; drop the percentage entirely (the amber line already says the
reading is uncertain); "2 readings" -> "two versions seen".

### 7. The Reworked diff on `#/changes` is one interleaved run of words — severity: medium

Defiance renders as a single paragraph: *"Increases ~~the~~ all threat
generated ~~by your attacks by 3%~~ ~~while~~ in Defensive ~~Stance.~~ stance
by an additional 5% while a shield is equipped."* Neither the Classic sentence
nor the Forever sentence can be read without mentally filtering every other
word, and this is the largest section on the page (127 talents, 25 on warrior
alone). Codex independently called it the worst thing on the page.

**Suggestion.** Two labelled lines - `Classic Era:` struck/greyed, `Forever:`
plain - with the diff as emphasis inside each, not one merged run. The data is
already there (`compactDiff` keeps the ops); only the renderer changes.

### 8. The "Moved" sentences have three shapes and contradict themselves — severity: medium

From one screen of `#/changes/warrior`:

- *"Improved Overpower - Moved from row 3 to row 2. Now in Arms."* (never left Arms; the second sentence is noise)
- *"Improved Slam - Moved from Fury, row 5 to row 6. Also reworked. Now in Arms."* (reads as though it moved to Fury row 6)
- *"Iron Will - Moved from Protection. Also reworked. Now in Fury."* (no rows at all)

**Suggestion.** One template, source and destination in one clause: *"Arms row
3 -> Arms row 2."*, *"Fury row 5 -> Arms row 6. Also reworked."* Emit "Now in
X" only when the tree actually changed, and never as a separate sentence.

### 9. "What's new" highlights 49 of 52 cells, which is the same as highlighting nothing — severity: medium

On paladin the diff is `new 21, moved 11, rank-changed 1, text-changed 13,
values-changed 3, same 3`. Turning the switch on dims exactly three cells
(screenshot `whatsnew-on.png`) and rings everything else in the same blue,
whether a talent is brand new or had one number nudged. The control's name
promises *new*, and the star marker in the cell corner does mean only `new`
(21 stars) - so star and ring disagree about what "new" is.

**Suggestion.** Either rename the control to "Changed since Classic" and give
the ring one colour per severity (new / moved / reworked), or keep the name and
scope the ring to `new` and `moved` only, which is 32 of 52 and still means
something.

### 10. The legend omits the marker that is on 40 % of cells — severity: medium

The legend explains the `?` and the rank badge. The blue star - the most
frequent marker on the grid, 21 cells on paladin - is not in it, and its
tooltip line only appears once a card is open.

**Suggestion.** Add it: *"★ = new in Forever."* Three words, same row.

### 11. A `sel=` deep link covers a third of the tree and eats the first click — severity: medium

`#/mage?sel=arcane-focus` pins a card that overlaps seven cells; `?sel=
arcane-subtlety` overlaps eleven. Clicking an *addable* covered cell
(`improved-channeling`) the first time only dismisses the card - rank stays 0,
`sel` drops from the hash. The second click adds the point. Since a `sel=` link
is what gets shared to point at a talent, the recipient's first interaction is
always the one that is swallowed. The card also has no close affordance: its
only buttons are the three nested terms.

**Suggestion.** Let the dismissing press fall through to the cell, or give a
pinned card a visible `x` (and it should be a pinned card only until the
pointer first leaves it).

### 12. Stickiness is invisible, and nothing advertises the dotted terms — severity: medium

`tt-plain.png` and `tt-sticky.png` are pixel-identical apart from the pointer:
once the card takes the pointer, nothing changes about it. And the only pointer
to the whole nested-card feature is *"Move the pointer into a tooltip to keep
it open, or press d"* buried in a 10.5 px paragraph under the trees; the dotted
underlines themselves are the same colour as the body text. The aria-labels are
excellent (*"How ranks 1 to 2 were derived"*, *"What Arcane Subtlety said in
Classic Era"*) and a sighted mouse user never sees them.

**Suggestion.** On sticky, brighten the card border by one step - it costs
nothing and tells the player the card is now theirs. Tint the dotted terms
(the change line's blue, the trust line's amber) so they read as links, and put
one short line in the legend: *"Underlined words in a tooltip open a card."*

### 13. Two controls with nearly the same name, side by side — severity: medium

The control row reads `Search | ★ What's new | What changed vs Classic |
Reset`. The first is an in-page dim toggle, the second navigates away to
`#/changes`. Nothing in the styling separates a toggle from a link.

**Suggestion.** Rename the toggle to "Highlight changes" and move the
`#/changes` entry into the site header beside `Changes · Races · Spells`, where
the other navigation already lives.

### 14. The shortcut help is printed twice on an empty build — severity: medium

On a fresh class page the summary column's "Worth knowing" list and the hint
paragraph under the trees both explain shift-click, ctrl-click, right click,
`/`, `d` and Ctrl+Z. Both are on screen at once, in different wordings
("Ctrl+click empties one" vs "Ctrl-Z undoes, Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes").

**Suggestion.** Keep the summary column's version (it is the better one and it
disappears on the first click) and cut the paragraph under the trees to the one
thing the overlay cannot teach: *"Press ? for all shortcuts."*

### 15. Two different "levels" in one panel, and the level sentence is clumsy — severity: medium

The summary heading says `Warrior 16/0/0 - level 25` (required level), the
slider below says `60`, and the sentence says *"At level 60 you have all 16 of
your 16 spent points."* Three level statements, no labels distinguishing
"level this build needs" from "level I am asking about". Codex flagged the same
contradiction from the print sheet alone.

**Suggestion.** Label the heading (`needs level 25`) and make the sentence
answer the question asked: at or above the required level, *"All 16 points are
available at level 60."*; below it, keep the existing *"31 of your 51 spent
points - 20 too many."*

### 16. Race and spell pages state the same caveat twice, adjacently — severity: medium

`#/races/dwarf` and `#/spells/mage` both carry, on consecutive lines:

> Read from BlizzCon 2026 footage; not yet reviewed.
> BlizzCon demo build 2026-09-12, read from stream video; nothing here is
> reviewed.

Then every trait card repeats an `unverified` pill (3 of 4 on Dwarf), and the
mage page adds three more caveat lines before the first spell. Six lines of
disclaimer above the content the player came for - the exact failure
round-one A1.3 named.

**Suggestion.** One sentence, once: *"Read from the BlizzCon 2026 demo on
12 September; nothing here has been checked by hand."* Drop the second line
entirely and let the per-card `unverified` pill go, since the page-level
sentence already covers it.

### 17. Race pages do not mark the combinations the matrix calls new — severity: medium

`#/races` gold-rings Dwarf Shaman and announces *"16 combinations are new since
Classic Era"*. `#/races/dwarf` lists `Can be Hunter Paladin Priest Rogue Shaman
Warrior` with Shaman styled exactly like the rest. The headline claim of the
section disappears on the page a player lands on.

**Suggestion.** Carry the gold ring onto the class chips, and add the count to
the race header: *"Dwarf - 6 classes, 1 new since Classic Era."*

### 18. The spells page presents mangled names as findings — severity: medium

`#/spells/mage` shows `Evocation Dampen Magic  Rank 3  [New]  Check this
reading.` - two spells merged into one row - and `Comprehend Scroll [New]`,
which is not a spell. The `New` chip means "this name is missing from our
Classic list", so a misread name is *guaranteed* to be labelled New. The page's
caveat ("It is a hint, not a finding") does not survive contact with a row that
is visibly wrong.

**Suggestion.** Suppress the `New` chip on any entry that is also flagged
uncertain - the two claims cancel out - and let those rows say only *"Name
unclear on screen."*

### 19. The class-page help line is 10.5 px — severity: medium

`getComputedStyle('.class-help').fontSize` is `10.5px` on the live build,
below the 12 px floor `docs/handover/2026-09-13-ui-polish-icons.md` section 2
says is now universal. It is also the only place the nested-tooltip feature is
explained (finding 12).

**Suggestion.** 12 px, and shorten the text per finding 14 so it still fits on
one line.

### 20. The derivation sentences are repetitive and read like arithmetic — severity: low

Nearly every mage card ends with a variant of *"Rank 1 was read from the
stream. Ranks 2-5 are rank 1 multiplied by the rank, the way the matching
Classic Era talent scales."* Where rounding applies it is spelled out twice in
one card: *"Rank 3 may read 40 in game; the value shown is the unrounded 42.
Rank 4 may read 55 in game; the value shown is the unrounded 56."* - and
"unrounded 42" is larger than the "rounded" 40, which reads as an error.
Separately, the trust term and the rank term open with the same sentence, so
two of the three nested cards say the same thing.

**Suggestion.** *"Only rank 1 was on screen. Higher ranks scale the way the
Classic Era talent did."* plus, once, *"In game these may show as 40 and 55."*
Give the trust term the confidence story and the rank term the numbers, with no
overlap.

### 21. "Values by rank: 15/15." — severity: low

Paladin `infusion-of-light` has an unknown rank 2, so the rank card prints
`Values by rank: 15/15.` The 15 is the cast-time window, not the effect, and
repeating it says "rank 2 changes nothing", which is precisely what the card's
next sentence denies.

**Suggestion.** When ranks above 1 are unknown, omit the series line rather
than repeating rank 1 into it.

### 22. Class and tree names are capitalised inconsistently — severity: low

- Import: *"A build link for mage."*, *"That is a priest build. Open priest first"* - lowercase class names, everywhere else they are capitalised.
- Tooltip: *"Requires 15 points in Holy Talents."* against the notice bar's *"Infusion of Light needs 15 points in Holy."* and the panel header's `Holy`.
- Arrows: `→` in tooltips (`5% → 17%`), `->` in the import report and the clamp notice (`5 -> 3`).
- Headings: `What changed against Classic Era` (page) vs `What changed vs Classic` (button).

**Suggestion.** Capitalise class names everywhere; pick one of "Holy" or "Holy
Talents" (the in-game wording is "Holy Talents", so change the notice bar);
use `→` throughout; use "vs Classic Era" in both places.

### 23. The shortcuts overlay repeats "(talent)" four times — severity: low

`Click - Add one point (talent)`, `Right click - Refund one point (talent)`,
`Shift+Click - Fill to the last rank (talent)`, `Ctrl+Click - Empty the talent
(Alt works too) (talent)`. The last one says "talent" twice in one row. `Home+
End` should be `Home / End`.

**Suggestion.** The group is already headed "SPENDING POINTS"; drop the suffix.

### 24. Header tagline starts with a stray dash, and the skip link names trees that are not there — severity: low

The header reads `Changes · Races · Spells  - Classic+ talent calculator,
unofficial and unreviewed` - the leading `- ` is a separator glued to the nav
and reads as a typo. The first focusable element on `#/changes`, `#/races` and
`#/spells` is *"Skip to the talent trees"*, and none of those pages has a tree.

**Suggestion.** Drop the dash (or make it a `·` like its neighbours). Make the
skip link's target and wording per-route: "Skip to the content".

### 25. Embed mode keeps Reset but not Undo, and shows `?` markers with no legend — severity: low

`#/warrior?v=1&t=35321&embed=1` renders cleanly, but each tree keeps its
`Reset` button while the embed drops the summary column, the undo buttons and
the shortcut hint - so a visitor can destroy the embedded build with one click
and has no way back. The `?` markers are still drawn (Arms row 4) with the
legend that explains them removed.

**Suggestion.** Hide the per-tree `Reset` in embeds, or pair it with the
existing `Build reset - Undo` affordance. Either drop the `?` markers in embed
mode or keep the one-line legend.

  the `Copy as text` block and the `#/changes` overview table are all good as
  they stand; the copy-as-text output pasted into a chat window reads exactly
  as intended.
- Mobile was out of scope and was not measured.
